Retention, deletion and recovery

Administrator actions require an explicit prototype policy. Production retention, residency, backup expiry, RPO and RTO remain owner decisions before live operation. Restores execute only in a new temporary isolated database; this is not a production restore.

Action fields and safeguards

Preview: record_id, backup_scope (unknown/none/until), optional backup_expires_at with timezone. Inspect originals, derivatives, indexes, pending work, delivery copies and residuals below. Delete: operation_id, confirm_record_id. Changed impact requires a new preview.

Snapshot: retention_minutes (1–10080). Exercise: snapshot_id, policy: {scope, failure_mode, recovery_target_minutes}. Credentials are excluded. All delivery stays held until reconciliation succeeds and an administrator submits Resume: exercise_id. Unknown backup/export copies and unsupported withdrawals remain explicit residuals.
